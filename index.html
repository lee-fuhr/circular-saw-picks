How we choose our picks

We do not run a test lab. Instead, we spend weeks reading through manufacturer specs, owner forums, video teardowns, and long-term reviews from trusted independent publishers. We look for patterns: what breaks after six months, which saws hold alignment, and which motors bog down in dense material. A saw earns our recommendation only when it has a clear reputation for reliability and real-world performance. We also gather feedback from pros and serious hobbyists who use these tools daily. If a model has a known weak point – flimsy base plates, poor dust ports, or switch problems – we flag it. We prioritize saws that offer the best combination of cut quality, ergonomics, and longevity for their price tier. No hype, just honest trade-offs.
