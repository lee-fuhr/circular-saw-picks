A good circular saw is the backbone of any serious woodworking or construction project. Whether you’re ripping plywood, crosscutting dimensional lumber, or trimming deck boards, the right saw makes the job faster, cleaner, and safer. When choosing, focus on power, weight, blade quality, and safety features like an electric brake. Corded models offer unlimited runtime and lower cost, while modern brushless cordless saws can rival corded performance if you’re already invested in a battery system. Best for specific needs

Best for heavy-duty job site use

If you need a saw that can handle framing all day without overheating or slowing down, the Makita 5007MG is the clear winner. Its magnesium construction keeps weight manageable, while the motor delivers the grunt necessary for thick lumber and repeated rip cuts. Pros report it holds up for years even in rough conditions. Our pick: Makita 5007MG.

Best for cordless jobs

For those who demand top-tier cutting performance without a cord, the Milwaukee 2630-20 sets the standard. It cuts fast and smooth, and the M18 battery platform offers excellent runtime. Just be prepared for the higher price and heavier weight, it’s a small trade for true cordless freedom on the job site. Our pick: Milwaukee 2630-20.

Best for budget DIY

When you need a capable saw without spending much, the Skil 5280-01 delivers surprising value. It’s light enough for overhead cuts and accurate for most home projects. While it won’t replace a pro-grade saw, it’s more than enough for weekend warriors and occasional repairs. Our pick: Skil 5280-01.

Our verdict

After poring over owner feedback and expert reviews, the Makita 5007MG remains our top pick for anyone who wants a corded saw that balances power, weight, and durability. If you need an electric brake or prefer cordless, the DeWalt DWE575SB and Milwaukee 2630-20 are excellent alternatives. And for tight budgets, the Skil 5280-01 proves that a good saw doesn’t have to be expensive. Whichever you choose, pair it with a quality blade, and you’ll be set for years of accurate cuts.
